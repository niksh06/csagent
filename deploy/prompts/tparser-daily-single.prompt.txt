TParser **daily digest** — single agent. Write the digest from the feed below.

**You do not fetch anything.** The `tparser-digest-feed` cron builtin has already
pulled the 24h window from the API, bucketed it by topic and chosen what the
digest covers — top-15% of each topic by `priority` (floor 10, cap 45) plus every
`alert` and every post with `urgency >= 0.70`. Selection is deterministic and
already done. Your job is the part a model is actually good at: what it means.

Do not call the API, do not read `.env`, do not run curl. There is no token to
handle here — earlier versions pasted it into shell commands and leaked it into
transcripts.

## Why selection is not yours to redo

`priority` is calibrated per rubric and is NOT comparable across them (measured
on 4361 posts / 5 days): Programming tops out at 0.79 and can never reach 0.80,
while AI Security's *median* is 0.81. The score is also clumped — 306 AI posts
share exactly 0.683. So there is no threshold you could apply that would mean
the same thing in two topics. Rank inside a topic is the only comparable
quantity, and the feed is already ranked. Take it as given.

## What to write

**Your entire final message IS the digest.** Its first character must be `📬`.
Everything you print is delivered verbatim — a line like "Данные собраны, ниже
дайджест" is not a note to yourself, it is the first thing he reads. Do not
announce what you are about to do, do not explain deviations, do not summarise
your own process. If a rule below made you choose differently, just choose —
silently. Russian.

**Tier 1 — глубокие блоки, ≤6.**
- One block for every topic that has at least one selected post — a topic with
  posts is never silent. Then fill the remaining slots with the highest-ranked
  posts left over, regardless of topic.
- **One story per Tier 1.** If a post covers a story already shown in another
  block (the same incident reported by a second channel), skip it and take the
  next distinct one. Six blocks about three stories is worse than six about six.
- Prefer `!`-flagged posts: they are alerts or high-urgency and must not be
  buried in the index.

**Tier 2 — индекс: every remaining selected post, one line each.**
Not a sample of them — all of them, grouped by topic, highest rank first. That
is the whole point of the feed: the count in the header must match what you
printed.

Deep block (**≤500 characters each**):

```
### {channel} · `{priority}` · {topic}
**Суть:** 1–2 предложения — главное утверждение, названные сущности.
**Вердикт:** 1–2 предложения — что это значит в разрезе tparser-workflow.
{tg link} · {external link or «нет URL»}
```

Index line (**≤130 characters each**):

```
`{priority}` {6–9 слов о сути} — {tg link}
```

Copy links character for character from the feed. Never rewrite a `t.me/c/…`
link, never use markdown link syntax for it, never invent one.

**Deep check** (optional, **≤2 per run**): for a Tier-1 post with an external
http(s) link and a thin summary, use `browser_navigate` + `browser_snapshot`
(browser-ops) on that one URL. Never invent content.

**Dedup:** window only; do not write `memory_fact_add` / `seen_post` facts. No
Obsidian writes.

## Rules

- Russian. **TL;DR first**, immediately after the header lines.
- **The header numbers come from the feed's «СЧЁТЧИКИ ДЛЯ ШАПКИ» block. Copy
  them. Do not recompute, do not round, do not estimate.** `{n1}`…`{n5}` are the
  per-topic «отобрано N из M» counts, also given in the feed.
- `{coverage}` says how much of the day this digest carries. It is deliberately
  a selection, not the whole day — printing the real number beats implying the
  day was small. If it drops sharply while `{total}` looks normal, something
  upstream broke and that is worth a line.
- `media_no_text` are posts with no caption at all (media, memes). They are
  counted, never summarised — there is nothing in them to summarise.
- If a topic has 0 selected posts: one line «нет постов за окно», no index
  section for it.
- Do not invent posts, links, or numbers.

If the feed contains no selected posts at all:
`📬 TParser · день · {дата} · релевантных постов не было.`
